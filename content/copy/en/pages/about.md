# Skincare, explained clearly.

CELDYQUE brings together concentrates, serums, moisturizers, cleansers and a scalp-care formula. Explore products by their format, ingredients and place in your routine.

## Understand the formula before adding a step

A familiar ingredient name is a starting point. The complete formula, directions and product format explain how a product is intended to be used. Our product pages and comparison guides bring those details together.

## Choose the format that fits

A concentrate may be mixed with a compatible moisturizer. A serum is a separate step. A cream is the moisturizer format. Cleansers are rinsed off, while the scalp serum is described as a leave-on product. Follow the directions for the exact formula you have.

## Find product information and support

Use the current packaging for the full ingredient declaration and the most up-to-date directions. For product questions, contact team@celdyque.com with the product name and, where relevant, batch details.

Purchases are completed with the selected retailer. That retailer handles its own pricing, delivery, payments and returns.
