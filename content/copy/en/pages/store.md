# Where to buy

Explore CELDYQUE through the retailer links listed below. Product selection, prices, delivery destinations and return terms may differ by retailer and region.

## Before ordering

Check the exact product name, size and listing. Use the product-specific Amazon link when you want to match a product on this site to its existing retailer destination.

## Your purchase is with the retailer

Checkout, payment, delivery and returns take place on the selected retailer's service. Consult that retailer's current terms and contact its support team about an existing order.

For questions about the product itself, [contact CELDYQUE](contact.html).
