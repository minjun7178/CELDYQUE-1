# ArbutXA Glow Cream

CELDYQUE ArbutXA Glow Cream is a moisturizer with Arbutin 5%, Alpha-Arbutin 2%, Tranexamic Acid 5% and Niacinamide 2%, applied after serum.

## Where it fits

Choose ArbutXA Glow Cream for the moisturizer step. Glutathione Glow Serum and Niacinamide 20% Pore Minimizer Serum are serum formats applied earlier in a routine.

## How to use

1. Apply a moderate amount after serum.
2. Spread evenly across the face and neck according to the packaging directions.
3. Follow the sunscreen directions for daytime use.

Use morning and evening as tolerated, as the cream step of your routine.

## Ingredient information

Formulated with Arbutin 5%, Alpha-Arbutin 2%, Tranexamic Acid 5% and Niacinamide 2%. Arbutin and Alpha-Arbutin are listed separately. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

## Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.
