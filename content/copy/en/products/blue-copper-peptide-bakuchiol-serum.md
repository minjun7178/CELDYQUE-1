# Blue Copper Peptide Bakuchiol Serum

CELDYQUE Blue Copper Peptide Bakuchiol Serum is a face serum used after cleansing and toning, before moisturizer.

## Where it fits

Add this serum between toner and cream. Compare the selected ingredients with those of other CELDYQUE serums, and check the full ingredient list on your packaging.

## How to use

1. Apply after cleansing and toning.
2. Apply 2–3 drops to the face and neck, then gently pat until absorbed.
3. Follow with moisturizer and use sunscreen as the final morning step.

Use morning and evening. Introduce gradually if active skincare ingredients are new to your routine.

## Ingredient information

Selected ingredients include Blue Copper Peptide, Bakuchiol, Matrixyl 3000, EGF, Argireline and Beta-Glucan. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

## Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.

## Ingredient research

Bakuchiol is studied for the appearance of fine lines and uneven pigmentation, while niacinamide is studied for uneven-looking tone. These are ingredient research areas, not a clinical comparison or a promised result for this peptide serum.
