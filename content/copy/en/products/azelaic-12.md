# Azelaic Acid 12% Topical Serum

CELDYQUE Azelaic Acid 12% Topical Serum is a face serum applied after toner and before moisturizer.

## Where it fits

Use it as a separate serum step. Read the complete ingredient list when choosing products for your routine, as this formula includes more than one highlighted ingredient.

## How to use

1. Apply after cleansing and toner, following the amount on your packaging.
2. Introduce the product gradually, following its directions.
3. Follow with moisturizer and use sunscreen during the day according to its label.

Introduce gradually and increase frequency as tolerated.

## Ingredient information

Selected ingredients include Azelaic Acid 12%, Salicylic Acid and Tranexamic Acid. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

## Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.

## Ingredient research

Azelaic acid has been studied in topical formulations for facial pigmentation, including a study of a 20% cream. This is a 12% serum with a different formula, so those findings are ingredient background rather than clinical results for this product.
