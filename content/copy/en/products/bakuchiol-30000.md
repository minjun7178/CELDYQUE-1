# Bakuchiol 30,000ppm Serum

CELDYQUE Bakuchiol 30,000ppm Serum is a mix-in concentrate for use with a compatible moisturizer, cream or serum.

## Where it fits

Mix the concentrate in your palm before application. Bakuchiol Volume Collagen Cream is a separate cream product applied as the moisturizer step.

## How to use

1. Add 1–2 drops to moisturizer, cream or a compatible serum, as directed on your label.
2. Blend in your palm immediately before use.
3. Apply the mixture as directed. Follow your sunscreen label for morning use.

Start slowly and patch test before first use. Use sunscreen in the morning.

## Ingredient information

Selected ingredients include 30,000 ppm Bakuchiol and Butylene Glycol. This concentrate is designed to mix with compatible skincare before use. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

## Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.

## Ingredient research

Bakuchiol has been studied in topical creams for the appearance of fine lines and uneven pigmentation. One trial used a 0.5% cream. This mix-in serum has a different formula and application method, so the trial does not establish the same outcome here.
