# Glutathione Glow Serum 30,000 ppm

CELDYQUE Glutathione Glow Serum 30,000 ppm is a lightweight face serum used before moisturizer.

## Where it fits

Apply this serum after cleansing and toning. ArbutXA Glow Cream is a cream-format option for the moisturizer step; choose the format that fits the step you need.

## How to use

1. Apply after cleansing and toning.
2. Apply 2–3 drops to the face as directed on your packaging; gently pat in.
3. Follow with moisturizer and use sunscreen in the morning according to its label.

Use daily, morning or evening, as tolerated.

## Ingredient information

Selected ingredients include 30,000 ppm Glutathione, Niacinamide and Adenosine. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

## Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.
