# Pore Purifying Cleansing Oil

CELDYQUE Pore Purifying Cleansing Oil features Centella, Tea Tree, Green Tea, Heartleaf and LHA. Apply to dry skin, emulsify with water and rinse off.

## Where it fits

Begin with dry hands and dry skin. Add water after massaging to emulsify the oil. Follow with Gentle Cleanser if you choose a second cleansing step.

## How to use

1. Apply 2–3 pumps to dry skin with dry hands.
2. Massage gently to loosen makeup, sunscreen and daily buildup.
3. Add water until milky, then rinse thoroughly. Follow with a foaming cleanser if desired.

Avoid direct eye contact. Rinse thoroughly if contact occurs.

## Ingredient information

Selected ingredients include Centella Asiatica, Tea Tree, Green Tea, Heartleaf and LHA. Massage onto dry skin, emulsify with water and rinse thoroughly. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

## Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.
