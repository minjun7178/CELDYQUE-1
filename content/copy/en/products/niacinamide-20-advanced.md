# Niacinamide 20% Pore Minimizer Serum

CELDYQUE Niacinamide 20% Pore Minimizer Serum is a face serum applied after toner and before moisturizer.

## Where it fits

Use it as the serum step in your routine. Follow the amount and frequency on your packaging and introduce it gradually.

## How to use

1. After cleansing and toning, apply a few drops to the face.
2. Introduce gradually, especially if niacinamide is new to your routine.
3. Follow with moisturizer and use sunscreen in the morning.

Start slowly and patch test before first use.

## Ingredient information

Selected ingredients include Niacinamide 20%, Damask Rose Water and Green Tea. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

## Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.

## Ingredient research

Niacinamide has been studied for the appearance of uneven tone, fine lines and skin texture. A controlled facial study used 5% niacinamide. A higher percentage does not by itself demonstrate a stronger or faster result.
