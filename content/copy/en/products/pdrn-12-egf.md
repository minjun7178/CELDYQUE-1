# PDRN 12% + EGF Peptide Serum

CELDYQUE PDRN 12% + EGF Peptide Serum is a face serum applied after cleansing and toning, before moisturizer.

## Where it fits

Use this serum as a separate step before your cream. PDRN 12% + EGF Peptide Cream is the moisturizer in the same collection; the two products have different roles.

## How to use

1. Apply after cleansing and toning.
2. Apply 2–3 drops over the face or intended area, following your product label.
3. Follow with moisturizer. Use sunscreen as the final morning skincare step according to its label.

Use daily as tolerated. Introduce gradually before making it a regular serum step.

## Ingredient information

The PDRN 12% name refers to a 120,000 ppm PDRN complex. Selected ingredients include EGF peptide, Volufiline and Niacinamide. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

## Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.

## Ingredient research

PDRN is investigated in laboratory skin-cell research. Niacinamide has been studied in topical formulations for the appearance of uneven tone and fine lines, while panthenol is studied for skin hydration. These ingredient roles help explain the formula without promising a recovery or treatment result.
