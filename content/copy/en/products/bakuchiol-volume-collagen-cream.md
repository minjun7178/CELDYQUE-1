# Bakuchiol Volume Collagen Cream

CELDYQUE Bakuchiol Volume Collagen Cream is a moisturizer applied after serum to the face and neck.

## Where it fits

Apply this cream as its own moisturizer step. It differs from Bakuchiol 30,000ppm Serum, a concentrate mixed with compatible skincare before application.

## How to use

1. Apply a moderate amount after serum.
2. Spread across the face and neck as directed.
3. Use as the moisturizer step; finish your morning routine with sunscreen according to its label.

Use morning and evening as the cream step of your routine.

## Ingredient information

Selected ingredients include 5,000 ppm Bakuchiol, Volufiline, EGF and Collagen. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

## Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.

Bakuchiol has been studied in topical formulations for smoother-looking skin and the appearance of fine lines and uneven pigmentation. The complete formula matters; an ingredient concentration alone does not establish that two creams will perform alike.
