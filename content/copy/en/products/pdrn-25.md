# PDRN 250,000ppm Concentrate

CELDYQUE PDRN 250,000ppm Concentrate is a gel-type concentrate in the PDRN collection, alongside the PDRN serum and cream.

## Where it fits

Choose between the concentrate, serum and cream by their application method and place in your routine. The serum is a separate step before moisturizer; the cream is the moisturizer step.

## How to use

1. Use after cleansing and toning on clean skin.
2. Apply a few drops directly or mix with a compatible serum or cream.
3. Follow with moisturizer. Use sunscreen in the morning.

Introduce gradually if concentrated active formulas are new to your routine.

## Ingredient information

Formulated with 250,000 ppm PDRN, EGF peptide and Panthenol in a gel-type concentrate. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

## Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.

## Ingredient research

PDRN has been studied for effects on skin-cell activity and collagen-related processes in laboratory cultures. Panthenol is studied for moisture support, with topical dexpanthenol research reporting improved skin hydration. Cell studies do not establish skin regeneration from a topical cosmetic.
