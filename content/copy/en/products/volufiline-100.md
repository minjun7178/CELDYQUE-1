# Volufiline 100% Concentrate

CELDYQUE Volufiline 100% Concentrate is an oil-based concentrate for mixing with a compatible cream, lotion or serum.

## Where it fits

Mix a small amount with your usual moisturizer in your palm immediately before application. This mix-in format differs from a serum applied as a separate step.

## How to use

1. Dispense 1–2 drops into your palm.
2. Mix with your usual cream, lotion or compatible serum.
3. Apply the mixture to the intended area, following the directions on your packaging.

Mix in your palm immediately before application. Follow the directions and precautions on your packaging.

## Ingredient information

The 100% describes Volufiline raw material used as the concentrate, not 100% pure sarsasapogenin. This is an oil-based mix-in product. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

## Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.
