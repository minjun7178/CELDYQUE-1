# Comparing CELDYQUE's tone-care serum and cream formats

ArbutXA Glow Cream, Glutathione Glow Serum and Niacinamide 20% are presented in the current CELDYQUE collection for tone-focused skincare. They differ in format, ingredient summary and routine position.

Begin with the step you need. A cream and a serum are not automatically substitutes, and a larger percentage on a label is not a ranking of product performance.

## Start with format and routine role

ArbutXA is the cream-format option. The current directions place it after serum as the moisturizer step.

Glutathione and Niacinamide are serum-format options. Their ingredient summaries differ, even though both appear before moisturizer. Check the full formula and directions before choosing which, if either, fits your routine.

There is no requirement to use all three together. A comparison guide should make a decision easier rather than add steps automatically.

## Read percentages in context

The current ArbutXA page lists Arbutin 7%, Tranexamic Acid 5% and Niacinamide 2%. The Glutathione name includes 30,000 ppm. The Niacinamide product name includes 20%.

Each figure needs a clear definition and current labeling support. Percentages for different ingredients do not form a common scale of effectiveness. Even for one ingredient, the rest of the formula and the use directions matter.

Do not turn those numbers into an unsupported claim that one product works faster, treats a condition or is suitable for every skin type.

## Set expectations from evidence

The current descriptions focus on the appearance of tone, radiance and texture. This guide does not promise removal of spots, a particular degree of lightening or a fixed result timeline.

Product-specific claims should be supported by evidence that actually applies to the finished formula. An ingredient study is not automatically a study of the product in your bottle.

Follow each product's current directions. The existing site includes sunscreen as the final morning skincare step; use it separately according to its own label.

## Common questions

### Is the cream a stronger version of the serum?

No such equivalence is established. These products have different ingredient summaries and formats.

### Should I begin several new active products together?

Introduce unfamiliar products according to their labels. Making several changes at once makes it harder to identify how an individual formula fits your routine.

### Where can I see every ingredient?

The selected summaries here are not complete formulas. Use the INCI declaration on current packaging and contact the brand if a specific ingredient matters to your choice.
