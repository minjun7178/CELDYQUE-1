# Cleansing oil and foaming cleanser: which step does each perform?

CELDYQUE Pore Purifying Cleansing Oil and Gentle Cleanser Purifying Touch are both rinse-off products, but their application methods differ. The oil starts on dry skin and is emulsified with water. The foaming cleanser is used as a water-based wash.

A useful comparison starts with these directions rather than assuming every routine needs two cleansers.

## Compare the two formats

| Detail | Pore Purifying Cleansing Oil | Gentle Cleanser Purifying Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Current listed size | 200 ml / 6.76 fl oz | 150 ml / 5.07 fl oz |
| Starting point | Dry hands and dry skin | Wet skin and hands |

| Routine role in current copy | Oil-cleansing step for makeup and sunscreen removal | Foaming cleansing step |

Follow the amount and method printed on your current packaging. This table summarizes the existing website directions rather than replacing the product label.

## Using the cleansing oil

Apply the directed amount with dry hands to dry skin. Massage gently, then add water to emulsify the oil before rinsing thoroughly. The current product page describes following with a foaming cleanser as optional.

Avoid assuming that a cleansing oil is a leave-on facial oil. Its rinse-off directions are an essential part of the product format.

## Using Gentle Cleanser

Wet the skin and hands, lather the amount directed on the packaging and massage gently. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.

The existing product name and description use “gentle,” but a name alone does not establish suitability for every skin type. Review the complete ingredients and your own product directions.

## Do you need both?

Choose the cleansing steps that suit what you need to remove and the directions for the products you use. If you choose both, the sequence described by the current site is oil first, followed by the foaming cleanser.

Using more steps is not automatically better. Neither the catalog nor this guide is a requirement to use the full range.

## Reading the ingredient information

The cleansing oil page highlights soybean oil, LHA and selected botanical ingredients. Other parts of the site highlight a different botanical selection. Gentle Cleanser's page mentions AHA, BHA and PHA categories, while its selected ingredient list does not fully identify those categories.

These summaries need to be checked against the current full INCI declarations. Do not treat a short ingredient highlight as a complete list or use it to rule out an ingredient you need to avoid.

## Questions before use

### Can I leave either cleanser on overnight?

No. Both products are described as rinse-off formulas. Follow the rinsing instructions.

### Is a double cleanse mandatory?

No. The current oil directions make the foaming step optional. Follow your product labels and cleansing needs.

### Is the current website a verified source for free-from claims?

The current oil page includes several free-from statements, but the supporting formula documentation has not been supplied for this package. Consult the current label and contact the brand for confirmation.
