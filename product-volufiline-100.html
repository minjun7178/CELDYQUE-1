Frequently Asked Questions

Format, directions and ingredient information for this product.

What type of product is Volufiline 100% Concentrate?
CELDYQUE Volufiline 100% Concentrate is an oil-based mix-in concentrate. Mix 1–2 drops with a compatible cream, lotion or serum, following your product label.
What does Volufiline 100% mean?
The 100% refers to the Volufiline raw material used as the concentrate, rather than diluted into a conventional water-based serum. It does not mean 100% pure sarsasapogenin. Mix 1–2 drops with a compatible cream, lotion or serum before application.
Does it replace moisturizer?
No. It is mixed with a compatible moisturizer or other skincare product before application; it does not replace the moisturizer step.
