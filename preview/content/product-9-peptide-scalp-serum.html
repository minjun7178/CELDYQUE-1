Where it fits

Part your hair to reach the intended scalp area, such as the parting, hairline or crown. Apply with the roll-on and leave on without rinsing.

How to use

  1. Part the hair to expose the intended area on a dry or towel-dried scalp.
  2. Roll the applicator directly across the hairline, crown or parting.
  3. Massage gently and leave on without rinsing.

Use daily on a dry or towel-dried scalp. Avoid broken or irritated skin.

Ingredient information

Selected ingredients include Rosemary 2%, Caffeine 1%, Niacinamide 2%, PDRN 0.1%, a peptide complex, BHA and Zinc PCA. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children. Do not apply to broken or irritated skin.
