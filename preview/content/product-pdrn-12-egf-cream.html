Where it fits

Choose the cream for the moisturizer step. If you also use PDRN 12% + EGF Peptide Serum, apply the serum first and the cream afterward, following each product label.

How to use

  1. Apply a moderate amount after serum.
  2. Spread over the face and neck as directed on your packaging.
  3. For morning use, follow with sunscreen according to its own directions.

Patch test before first use. Use sunscreen as the final morning skincare step.

Ingredient information

Formulated with 120,000 ppm PDRN, EGF peptide and Volufiline in a cream format. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.

Ingredient research

Niacinamide has been studied for the appearance of uneven tone and fine lines. Panthenol is studied for moisture support. PDRN research also includes laboratory skin-cell studies; these do not establish wound-healing or post-procedure benefits from this cream.

Research sources

  • Bissett et al. — Niacinamide and facial skin appearance
  • Gehring and Gloor — Dexpanthenol, hydration and barrier function
  • PDRN research in cultured skin cells

Selected ingredients

Sodium DNA / PDRN, Volufiline, Niacinamide, Ceramide NP, Panthenol, Hyaluronic acid, sh-Oligopeptide-1 / EGF, Jojoba seed oil, Squalane.

Product questions

Where does it go in a routine?

Apply it as the cream or moisturizer step after serum, following your product label.

Is it the same product as the PDRN serum?

No. PDRN 12% + EGF Peptide Serum and PDRN 12% + EGF Peptide Cream are separate products with different routine steps. Use the directions for the product you have.

Can it be used with the PDRN serum?

Apply the serum first and the cream afterward, following each product label. Introduce unfamiliar products gradually.

Can I use it after a cosmetic procedure?

Follow your treating clinician’s advice about when to resume cosmetics after a procedure, and follow the precautions on your packaging.
