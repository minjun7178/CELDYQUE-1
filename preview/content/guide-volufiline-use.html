How to use CELDYQUE Volufiline 100% Concentrate

CELDYQUE Volufiline 100% Concentrate is presented as an oil-based mix-in product. The current directions recommend adding 1–2 drops to a compatible moisturizer, cream, lotion or serum before application.

Its format is the starting point: use the directions for a concentrate rather than assuming it works like a ready-to-use face serum.

A simple three-step method

  1. Dispense: Put 1–2 drops into your palm, following the directions on your bottle.
  2. Mix: Combine with a compatible cream, lotion, moisturizer or serum immediately before application.
  3. Apply: Spread the mixture over the intended area according to the current label.

The current page describes facial, neck and body uses. Check your own packaging for the intended areas and avoid direct eye contact. If the packaging instructions differ from the website summary, use the current product directions.

What does “100%” describe?

“100%” appears in the product name. It should not be read, without the supporting formula information, as a declaration that the product consists of one isolated active molecule.

The existing page describes a Volufiline raw material in an oil-based format. To understand the composition, consult the current INCI declaration and the brand's confirmed explanation of the labeling basis. The product name does not replace that information.

Choosing a mixing product

The directions refer to a compatible moisturizer, lotion, cream or serum. Compatibility is a property of the complete formulas, not just their ingredient names. Follow the product directions if they specify a particular type of base.

Do not mix other skincare into sunscreen. Apply sunscreen separately according to its own directions.

There is no need to replace your entire routine simply to add a concentrate. First establish how the product is intended to be used with the step you already have.

Expectations and product information

The existing product description positions the concentrate for firmness and fuller-looking skin care. This guide does not promise a measured increase in volume, an injection-like effect or a fixed time to results. Ingredient research and finished-product results are different kinds of evidence.

If you need to check a specific ingredient or a product batch, use the packaging declaration and contact the brand with the product name and batch details.

Frequently asked questions

Does the concentrate replace moisturizer?

The current directions describe mixing it into a compatible product. It is not presented as a replacement for the moisturizer used in that method.

Can I use more drops for a faster result?

The current method specifies 1–2 drops. Do not assume that using more improves the result; follow the directions on your bottle.

Is the complete ingredient list on the current website?

The existing page describes the raw material and oil-based format but refers readers to the package for the complete INCI. Use the package declaration when you need the full formula.
