Where it fits

Use it for the foaming wash step. Pore Purifying Cleansing Oil starts on dry skin and requires water to emulsify; the two products have different application methods.

How to use

  1. Dispense a small amount and lather with water.
  2. Gently massage over a damp face, avoiding the eye area.
  3. Rinse thoroughly and follow with your skincare routine.

Use morning and evening. Reduce frequency if your skin feels dry.

Ingredient information

Selected ingredients include Centella Asiatica, Tea Tree, Green Tea, and AHA, BHA and PHA. This is a rinse-off foaming cleanser. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.

Product questions

Is this left on the skin?

No. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water after washing.

How is it different from cleansing oil?

Gentle Cleanser is a foaming wash used on wet skin. Pore Purifying Cleansing Oil starts on dry skin, then is emulsified with water and rinsed off.

How often should I use it?

Use morning and evening. Reduce frequency if your skin feels dry.

Should I start on wet or dry skin?

Wet your face and hands before lathering Gentle Cleanser and massaging gently.
