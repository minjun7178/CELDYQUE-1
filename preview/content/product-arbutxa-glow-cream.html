Where it fits

Choose ArbutXA Glow Cream for the moisturizer step. Glutathione Glow Serum and Niacinamide 20% Pore Minimizer Serum are serum formats applied earlier in a routine.

How to use

  1. Apply a moderate amount after serum.
  2. Spread evenly across the face and neck according to the packaging directions.
  3. Follow the sunscreen directions for daytime use.

Use morning and evening as tolerated, as the cream step of your routine.

Ingredient information

Formulated with Arbutin 5%, Alpha-Arbutin 2%, Tranexamic Acid 5% and Niacinamide 2%. Arbutin and Alpha-Arbutin are listed separately. Read the full ingredient declaration on your packaging before use.

Before use

For external use only. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Patch test before first use and discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.

Ingredient research

Niacinamide is studied in topical skincare for the appearance of uneven tone and fine lines. Research on one ingredient or another formulation does not establish how quickly this cream will change the appearance of dark spots.

Product questions

Is this a serum or a cream?

ArbutXA Glow Cream is a moisturizer with Arbutin 5%, Alpha-Arbutin 2%, Tranexamic Acid 5% and Niacinamide 2%. Apply it after serum as the cream step.

How does it differ from Glutathione Glow Serum?

ArbutXA Glow Cream is the moisturizer format. Glutathione Glow Serum 30,000 ppm is a face serum applied before moisturizer. Its product page is linked below.

Can I use it morning and evening?

Yes. Use as tolerated after serum as the cream step. Follow with sunscreen in the morning.
